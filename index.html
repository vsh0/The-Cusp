What is Clipperton island?

Clipperton Island is a remote and relatively little-known island in the Pacific Ocean. Cusp of Discovery aims to promote awareness of this unique and isolated island. Clipperton Island is home to a variety of unique species, including the endemic Clipperton crab and several seabird species. The island's isolation has allowed for the development of distinct ecosystems that are of great interest to scientists and conservationists.
